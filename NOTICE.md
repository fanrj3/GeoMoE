# Notices

GeoMoE builds on the dual-encoder training design introduced by Sample4Geo.
The model, multi-scale datasets, routed FFN implementation, hierarchical search,
and path residual calibrator in this repository contain substantial project-specific
extensions. Retain attribution to Sample4Geo and its ICCV 2023 paper when
redistributing derived code.

Dataset images are not included. VIGOR-M and JustZoomIn remain governed by their
own terms. JustZoomIn includes Mapillary-derived imagery under CC BY-SA 4.0 and
